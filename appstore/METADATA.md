**Notlar**
```
BUILD 4 — FIX FOR THE PREVIOUS REJECTION (2.1(a), launch crash on iPadOS 27)
The app now adopts the UIScene lifecycle, which iOS/iPadOS 27 requires for apps built with the iOS 27 SDK. Launch was verified on iPad Air 11-inch (M3) and iPhone. Thank you for the report.

Ruhnevâz is a free, non-commercial Islamic companion app (Turkish and English). No account, no login, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics.

HOW TO TEST
- On first launch choose a language, then a city. "Use my location" is optional; any city can be searched manually, and the app works fully if location is denied.
- Home: next prayer countdown, today's prayers tracker, verse of the day. Tap the cards under "Explore" to reach the Qur'an, hadith, dhikr/dua, qibla, holy days, imsakiye and qada tracker. Settings is the sliders icon at the top right; About and the privacy policy are inside Settings.

PERMISSIONS
- Location (When In Use only): prayer times for the current city and the qibla direction. Rounded coordinates (2 decimals) are sent to api.aladhan.com only to calculate prayer times; nothing is stored or linked to the user. The qibla is computed on-device.
- Notifications: local prayer-time reminders, requested only when the user turns reminders on (onboarding or Prayer settings). No push server.

BACKGROUND AUDIO
- UIBackgroundModes "audio" is used so ayah-by-ayah Qur'an recitation (Qur'an > open a surah > play) keeps playing when the screen is locked. It is not used for anything else.

CONTENT & SOURCES
- The Qur'an Arabic text (Tanzil), the Diyanet Turkish translation and the Bukhari/Muslim hadith collections are bundled for offline use. Audio (cdn.islamic.network) and four other hadith collections (fawazahmed0/hadith-api) stream from public open-source APIs. All quotations show surah/ayah or hadith references; full credits are in Settings > About.

WIDGETS
- iOS 17+ Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets: "Next prayer" and "Daily verse". Long-press the Home Screen > + > Ruhnevâz.
```
